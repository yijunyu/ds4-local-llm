How it works

Tell the app what matters — speed, quality, context length — and it detects your chip, GPU, and unified memory, then picks the best model and execution mode that actually fits. One button: Download & Run. No terminal, no config.

Model weights are large open-source downloads (a few GB up to ~80 GB depending on your Mac). The app shows the exact size and asks before downloading. Requires an Apple Silicon Mac.
