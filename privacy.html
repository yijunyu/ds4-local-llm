Last updated: 2026-07-02

Ds4 Local LLM is designed so that your data never leaves your Mac. All AI processing runs locally on your device using Apple's GPU. We do not operate any server that receives your prompts, conversations, or files.

What we collect

Nothing. Ds4 Local LLM collects no personal data, has no user accounts, and includes no analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry. Your prompts, chats, and any files you open stay on your Mac.

Network use

The only times the app uses the internet:

  1. Model download. When you choose to download a model, the app fetches the weights directly from the publisher's servers (e.g. Hugging Face) over HTTPS. We receive nothing about you. You are shown the size and prompted before any download.
  2. Local API server (optional). If you enable it, the app serves an OpenAI-compatible API on your own machine (localhost / your local network, your choice) so your own tools can use the local model. This traffic stays on your device / local network; it is not sent to us.

There is no other network activity. The app functions fully offline once a model is downloaded.

Your files

The app only accesses files you explicitly choose (via the system open panel) or the model files it downloads into its own app container. It runs inside Apple's App Sandbox.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties (we have no data to share). Model weights you download are governed by their own publishers' terms and licenses.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
