Requirements

  • An Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later).
  • Free disk space for the model you choose (a few GB up to ~80 GB).
  • More unified memory runs larger, higher-quality models; the app auto-fits the best model for your Mac.

Common questions

Where do my prompts go? Nowhere — all inference runs on your Mac, and the engine that reads your prompts is code-signed without network access, so it cannot send them anywhere. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly opt in to sharing anonymous speed measurements or a reviewed list of your own preferences; both are off until you turn them on, and neither ever includes prompts, conversations or files. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an internet connection? Only once, to download a model. After that the app works fully offline.

Why is the model download large? Open-source LLM weights are large data files. The app shows the exact size and asks before downloading.

Can I use it with my coding tools? Yes — it exposes an OpenAI-compatible API on localhost you can point Claude Code, OpenCode, or any OpenAI-style client at.
